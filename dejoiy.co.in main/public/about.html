About DEJOIY

We build the joyful side of business.

DEJOIY India Private Limited is a technology company from New Delhi. We run a marketplace, BPO teams and the software that connects commerce and operations.

Mission

Reliable digital infrastructure that makes commerce simple.

We build platforms that take complex operations and make them fast and simple for business users.

Vision

One connected ecosystem for every business.

Any business, any size, any country, with access to the same tools the biggest companies use.

Our story

Tech should serve every business, not just the big ones.

DEJOIY started with one belief: the gap between enterprise tools and what small and mid-size businesses can use is too big, and a well-built platform can close it.

From our headquarters in India we build for India first and for the world next.

The pieces are connected on purpose. The marketplace moves commerce, the business portal runs operations, and Dmail and Joi keep everyone talking.
