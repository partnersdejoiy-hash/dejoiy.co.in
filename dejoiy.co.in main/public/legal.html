Business & BPO

For BPO teams and software projects through business.dejoiy.com.

Extra terms

  • Each engagement starts with a quote or service order that sets the scope, price, timeline and any service levels. That document takes priority over the general Terms.
  • Invoices are due within the period stated on them. We may pause work on overdue accounts after giving notice.
  • Unless your service order says otherwise, you own the deliverables you've paid for in full; we keep our own pre-existing tools and know-how.
  • Both sides keep each other's confidential information private, during and after the engagement.

Extra privacy

  • When our teams handle your customers' data for you, we act on your instructions as your processor, under a data processing agreement on request.
  • Our agents only access the systems and data a project needs, and access ends when the project does.
Dmail

Custom-domain email for teams at mail.dejoiy.com.

Extra terms

  • Plans, mailbox limits and prices are shown on the Dmail plans page. New accounts get an 11-day free trial; after that, the plan you choose is billed monthly or yearly.
  • Your company's admins control its domains, mailboxes, roles and policies, and can suspend or delete user accounts.
  • No bulk unsolicited email. Accounts that send spam or phishing may be suspended to protect everyone's delivery.
  • When a plan ends, we keep mailbox data for a short grace period so you can export it, then delete it.

Extra privacy

  • We store your emails, contacts and calendar to provide the service, and we don't read them except to fix a problem you report, keep the service secure, or when the law requires it.
  • Email to other domains is sent through an email delivery provider, which sees the message as it passes through.
  • Your company's admins can see account details and, depending on their settings, audit logs for their domain.
Joi AI

DEJOIY's AI mascot at joi-ai.dejoiy.com and inside our apps.

Extra terms

  • Joi is an AI and can make mistakes. Check important answers before you act on them.
  • Don't use Joi to create harmful, illegal or misleading content, or to get around the rules of any app.
  • When Joi is added to another website, that site's owner chooses what Joi can see there.

Extra privacy

  • Your messages are sent to the AI model providers that power Joi so she can reply. We don't let them use your chats to train their models where we can control it.
  • Joi can remember things you tell her to make later chats better. You can clear your chats and Joi's memory of you from your account.
  • If you let Joi use Dmail or Shine for you, she reads only what she needs for the task you asked for.
  • Joi sign-in uses one-time codes sent by email.
Shine

Chat and collaboration for teams, connected to Dmail.

Extra terms

  • Your company's admins manage who can use Shine, which channels exist, and whether people can message other companies.
  • Messages to large groups (such as everyone in a company) are for work announcements only.

Extra privacy

  • We store your chats, files, status and presence (active, away, offline) to run Shine.
  • Your profile photo and name come from your company's Dmail directory.
  • Admins can manage and export their organisation's data as their policies allow.
Careers
