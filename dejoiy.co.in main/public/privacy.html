1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how DEJOIY India Private Limited ("DEJOIY", "we") handles personal data across our Platforms: dejoiy.co.in, business.dejoiy.com, Dmail, Joi, Shine, our careers and internship sites, and our BPO and software services.

The DEJOIY Marketplace (dejoiy.com) has its own privacy policy for shopping and selling there.

We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. The Legal hub lists extra details for each app.

2. What we collect

  • What you give us: your name, email, phone number, company and messages, for example in a contact form, a sign-up, a job application or a service order.
  • Account data: login details, profile photo, settings and preferences.
  • Content you create: emails in Dmail, chats in Shine, conversations with Joi, and files you upload.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment partners. We keep billing records such as invoices and plan history, not your full card details.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited and error logs, so the Platforms run safely.
  • Data from your company: if your employer gives you a Dmail or Shine account, they share your name, work email and role with us.

3. Why we use it

  • to provide the Platforms and the services you asked for;
  • to keep accounts secure, for example sending sign-in codes and spotting misuse;
  • to reply to your messages and give support;
  • to bill you for paid services and meet tax and legal duties;
  • to improve the Platforms, using aggregated or de-identified data where we can;
  • to send service updates, and marketing only if you've agreed to it.

We rely on your consent, on what's needed to provide a service you asked for, or on a legal duty. You can withdraw consent at any time; it won't affect what we did before.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the Platforms, such as hosting, email delivery, payments and AI model providers, under contracts that limit what they can do with it;
  • your company, if your account is managed by your employer (its admins can manage your work account);
  • authorities, when the law requires it;
  • a buyer or successor, if DEJOIY or a Platform is merged or sold, under the same protections.

Some providers may process data outside India. When they do, we use providers and safeguards that meet Indian law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose we collected it for, or as long as the law requires (for example, invoices for tax records). When you close an account we delete or anonymise its data within a reasonable time, except what we must keep by law.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, where we don't need to keep it;
  • withdraw consent you gave us;
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself.

Email connect@dejoiy.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. If your account is managed by your company, we may pass your request to them.

7. Keeping it safe

We use encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring to protect personal data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Cookies and local storage

Our Platforms use cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember settings and understand how pages are used. You can clear or block them in your browser, but some features may stop working.

9. Children

Our Platforms aren't meant for children under 18 without a parent or guardian. We don't knowingly collect children's data without verifiable parental consent. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we'll tell you about significant changes before they take effect.
